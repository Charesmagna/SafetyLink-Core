## 1. Statutory Emergency Services Disclaimer
**CRITICAL NOTICE:** SafetyLink is a secondary sequential notification and escalation mesh network designed to alert private security responders, campus patrol networks, designated emergency contacts, and corporate safety nodes. 

1. **Not a Replacement for Statutory Emergency Services:** SafetyLink is NOT a replacement for government emergency services. In life-threatening emergencies where direct voice access to statutory dispatchers is available, users are advised to dial **10111 (Police)**, **10177 (Ambulance)**, or **112 (Cellular Emergency)** directly.
2. **Sequential Dispatch Network:** SafetyLink automatically attempts cellular SMS, USSD, and telephony voice routing in sequence. Successful delivery is dependent on cellular carrier network availability, signal strength, and recipient device states.

In compliance with the **Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013 - POPIA)**:

1. **Background GNSS / GPS Polling:** SafetyLink collects and processes precise geographic coordinates while running in the foreground, background, or when the screen is locked, solely for the purpose of broadcasting incident locations during distress activations.
2. **Acoustic and Environmental Evidence:** When a panic event is confirmed, SafetyLink may capture ambient audio recordings and network telemetry to package into a cryptographic evidence ledger for responder situational awareness.
3. **Data Subject Rights:** Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their location logs and contact rosters at any time through the in-app Confidential Vault or by contacting the system administrator.
